About the community

Blueprint Arcade

A fan site for people who love building in games. Share your creations, discover other builders’ work, and compare the games that make it possible.

Who it is for

Blueprint Arcade is for architecture and build-game players: builders who want to show their work, find ideas, and learn about games such as Welcome to Bloxburg, The Sims 4, House Flipper, Cities: Skylines II, Manor Lords, Tiny Glade, Hometopia, and Minecraft.
